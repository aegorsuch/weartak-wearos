# Position log (incomplete implementation)

Position logging is separate from application diagnostics and is disabled by default.
Enabling it in Developer Options records the watch UID and GNSS fixes in app-private
storage. Export is explicit; the CSV contains sensitive location history.
Storage retains at most 50,000 fixes, discarding older entries and flagging the
log as incomplete when retention removes entries.

The timestamp is the Android fix time in UTC (ISO 8601). Coordinates are WGS84
decimal degrees. `horizontal_accuracy_m` is Android `Location.accuracy`, the
estimated 68% horizontal confidence radius in metres, not ground-truth error;
it is empty when unavailable.

This is not yet a complete evaluation log: bearer and relay are blank and
disposition is `unknown`. A transport handoff is not confirmed receipt. The
recorder observes active Android location callbacks, not fixes generated by
hardware or other devices outside those callbacks. Repeated callbacks for the
same provider, fix timestamp, elapsed time and coordinates are deduplicated.
The incomplete-storage warning persists after a queue or storage failure; an
export remains available for recovering the records that were saved. Queued
fixes may be lost on abrupt process termination.

The current export is a CSV through the system document picker. TAK data-package
creation, TAK Server upload, offline transfer, and per-route delivery tracking
are not implemented.
